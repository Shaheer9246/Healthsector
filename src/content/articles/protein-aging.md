## The Protein Paradox of Aging

For decades, higher protein intake has been recommended for older adults. Emerging research reveals a nuanced picture: **moderate protein restriction may activate longevity pathways** that high-protein diets suppress.

### Key Findings

A 2026 meta-analysis in *Nature Aging* found adults over 65 consuming 0.8g/kg/day (vs 1.2g/kg) showed:

-   **23% increase** in autophagy markers
-   **Reduced mTOR activation** linked to accelerated aging
-   **Preserved insulin sensitivity** without significant muscle loss

> "It's not about deficiency — it's about timing and source," explains Dr. Maria Cohut, PhD. "Excess protein beyond utilization capacity may accelerate cellular senescence."

### Practical Implications

| Age Group | Recommended Intake | Timing Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 50–65 | 1.0–1.2 g/kg/day | Even distribution |
| 65+ | 0.8–1.0 g/kg/day | Higher at lunch, lower at dinner |
| Frail/Elderly | 1.2–1.5 g/kg/day | Medical supervision required |

*Always consult your healthcare provider before dietary changes.*
