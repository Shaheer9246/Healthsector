## Key Findings

A prospective cohort study of 12,000 adults found that 2-3 cups of black coffee daily correlated with:

-   **8% lower** visceral adipose tissue volume
-   **3% higher** lean muscle mass preservation
-   Effects remained significant after adjusting for exercise, diet quality, and sleep

### Mechanisms Proposed

Researchers suggest chlorogenic acids in coffee may enhance fat oxidation and reduce glucose absorption. Caffeine's thermogenic effect appears secondary to these polyphenol-mediated pathways.

> "This isn't a license to add sugar and cream," notes Dr. Yasemin Sakay. "The benefits were observed exclusively with black or minimally sweetened coffee."
